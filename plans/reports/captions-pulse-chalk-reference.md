## CHALK Template

### Core Aesthetic
Warm, personal, handmade. Chalkboard/classroom energy. Educational, mentorship, lifestyle. Soft, playful, professional-but-friendly.

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Brush pen / handwriting font (NOT serif, NOT strict sans; cursive/script-like qualities)
- **Weight/Case:** Medium to bold brush stroke; all-caps use depends on handwriting style chosen (informal, mixed case likely)
- **Size Behavior:** Varies with hand-drawn nature; no strict sizing rules; more organic scale

### Caption Animation
- **Word Entrance:** Stop-motion handwriting; each word/phrase animates as if being **written in real-time** with pen strokes
- **Timing Feel:** Slow, deliberate, human-paced; word appears character-by-character or in fluid writing motion
- **Effect Quality:** Nostalgic, pedagogical; feels authentic (not robotic)

### Keyword Emphasis
- **Color Highlight:** Not explicitly described; inferred as warm accent (ochre, rust, warm gold, or warm brown) OR same handwriting color with larger/bolder treatment
- **Size Change:** Keywords likely drawn larger or with bolder stroke weight
- **Treatment:** Playful outlines/doodles around keywords; illustrative emphasis

### Color Palette & Background
- **Text Color:** Warm earth tones OR muted colors (chalk-like: cream, pale yellow, warm white on warm bg)
- **Keyword Accent:** Warm color palette (ochre, rust, warm tan, or secondary warm tone)
- **Background:** Chalkboard-like OR paper texture (NOT black; likely warm/neutral like tan, beige, warm gray, or soft cream)
- **Plate/Box:** Textured background suggests chalkboard surface or paper, not solid color
- **Texture/Overlay:** Paper grain, chalk dust, or chalkboard surface texture visible (subtle, not harsh)

### Layout & Framing
- **Full-Frame Treatment:** Full-width captions, likely mid-to-lower third, overlaid on talking head footage
- **Layered Text:** Multiple text layers positioned behind speaker (depth, overlap)
- **Doodle/Sketch Overlays:** Playful hand-drawn shapes (underlines, circles, arrows, brackets) accent keywords and frame composition
- **B-Roll Insets:** Not confirmed; focus on doodle and text integration
- **Grid/Texture Overlay:** Chalkboard/paper texture baked into background treatment

### Overall Vibe
Approachable, trustworthy, educational. Used for tutorials, coaching, mentorship, lifestyle advice. Feels like learning from a friend, not a corporate entity.

### Confidence Level
Texture and handwriting animation confirmed; colors inferred from "warm" descriptor and chalkboard metaphor.

## REPLICATION CHECKLISTS

### PULSE Replication Checklist
- [ ] **Font:** Select clean geometric sans-serif (Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, or equivalent)
- [ ] **Text Color:** Solid white or very light gray (#FFFFFF or #F5F5F5)
- [ ] **Background:** Solid black or dark gray (#000000 or #1A1A1A); no texture
- [ ] **Keyword Accent:** Neon glow effect (CSS `text-shadow` or `filter: drop-shadow`); colors: cyan (#00FFFF), magenta (#FF00FF), lime (#00FF00), or bright white
- [ ] **Animation:** Word-by-word pop/slide entrance with fast timing (100–150ms per word, or sync to audio beat)
- [ ] **Playful Shapes:** Render geometric accent shapes (circles, bars, angled lines) positioned behind or around text
- [ ] **Layout:** Full-width text, bottom-third anchor; tight margins
- [ ] **Stroke:** Optional: thin 1–2px white stroke on text for anti-aliasing
- [ ] **Vibe Test:** Play video; confirm "hype," "broadcast," "digital-native" feel

### CHALK Replication Checklist
- [ ] **Font:** Select brush pen or handwriting font (Caveat, Comfortaa, Indie Flower, Brush Script, or equivalent; NOT geometric sans)
- [ ] **Text Color:** Warm earth tones (cream #F5E6D3, pale yellow #FFF8DC, or warm white #FFFAF0)
- [ ] **Background:** Warm/neutral textured surface simulating chalkboard or paper (tan #D2B48C, warm beige #F5DEB3, soft cream with noise filter)
- [ ] **Keyword Accent:** Warm secondary color (ochre #CC8811, rust #8B4513, warm brown #A0522D) OR larger brush stroke weight for same color
- [ ] **Animation:** Stop-motion handwriting entrance; text appears as if pen is drawing it in real-time (stroke animation or char-by-char fade with slight delay between characters)
- [ ] **Doodles/Outlines:** Hand-drawn playful shapes (underlines, circles, check marks, arrows) around or under keywords; layer behind text for depth
- [ ] **Layered Text:** Position multiple text lines at different depths; overlap permitted for visual richness
- [ ] **Texture Overlay:** Subtle paper grain or chalk dust overlay on entire caption area (opacity 10–20%)
- [ ] **Layout:** Full-width captions, mid-to-lower third; integrated with talking head
- [ ] **Vibe Test:** Play video; confirm "warm," "personal," "educational," "approachable" feel
